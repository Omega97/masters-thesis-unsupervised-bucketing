
# Graduation — Documents

Map of contents for the `Graduation/` folder: UNITS (University of Trieste) thesis and graduation requirements, deadlines, checklists, and official forms for the DSAI master's degree.

- **[[Graduation Notes]]** — *Note (UNITS).* Generic university graduation rules: thesis requirements, pre-graduation seminar (20–25 min), final discussion (8–10 min), grade calculation, and autumn 2026 session deadlines (23 Oct / 11 Dec).
- **[[Thesis Notes]]** — *Note (DSAI program).* **Authoritative for this degree** — DSAI-specific thesis process from [dsai.units.it](https://dsai.units.it/index.php/final-exam-2/): supervision rules, pre-laurea (40–45 min), laurea (10–12 min), committee composition, and mark calculation.
- **[[TODOs per Laurearsi]]** — *Checklist.* Actionable graduation to-do list: Esse3 thesis upload, hard-copy delivery, title-page template, and online application window (24 Aug – 11 Sep 2026). Targets the **23 October 2026** session.
- **[[DSAI Notes]]** — *Misc.* Personal program notes (CV tips, course follow-ups, tooling todos) — not graduation bureaucracy, but kept here for convenience.

> **DSAI vs UNITS timings:** [[Thesis Notes]] overrides [[Graduation Notes]] for presentation lengths and committee rules. Use Graduation Notes and [[TODOs per Laurearsi]] for Esse3 deadlines and bureaucratic steps.

### `UNITS-Documents/`

Official university forms and reference exports. PDFs exist locally but are gitignored (`*.pdf`); re-download from UNITS if missing after a fresh clone.

- **`units_CdL_laurea-frontespizioTesi_it.doc`** — *Word template.* Italian **thesis title page** template — frontespizio to follow when formatting the bound/PDF thesis.
- **`units_CdL_laurea-moduloIntegrativo_en.pdf`** — *PDF form.* **Supplementary Graduation Application** (English). Submit online via Esse3 during the application window (from 24 August 2026).
- **`ABPrivacyPolicyConsensiForm.txt`** — *Text export.* Plain-text snapshot of the [UNITS graduation specific information](https://degree.units.it/en/0320107313600001/students-area/graduation/graduation-specific-information) page — examination procedure, session dates, and links to related forms. (Filename is misleading; content is graduation info, not a privacy consent form.)
- **`privacyPolicy_INFO_679.txt`** — *Text.* Italian **GDPR privacy notice** (Reg. UE 2016/679) for student data processing at the University of Trieste — purposes, retention, and data-subject rights.
- **`privacyPolicy_INFO_679.pdf`** — *PDF.* PDF version of the same GDPR privacy notice.
