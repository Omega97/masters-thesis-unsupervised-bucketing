## World Models

**Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018** · [arXiv:1803.10122](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122) · [worldmodels.github.io](https://worldmodels.github.io)  
**Notes**: [[World Models 1803_10122v4 - paper notes]] 
**PDF**: `papers/World Models 1803.10122v4.pdf`

### Summary

Foundational paper that re-launched the modern idea of **world models**. The agent is split into a large **world model** (perception + dynamics) and a small **controller** (policy). The world model is trained unsupervised on random rollouts; the controller is trained entirely inside the "dream" the world model generates — and the resulting policy **transfers successfully to the real environment**. A surprising result that reframes credit assignment onto a compact search space.

### Experiments

- **CarRacing** (OpenAI Gym): ~10k random rollouts to train the latent space; controller trained in the dream world; strong driving performance with far fewer real-world samples.
- **VizDoom**: same framework applies (extended in follow-up work).

### Key insight & limitation

> *"We can even train our agent entirely inside of its own hallucinated dream generated by its world model, and transfer this policy back into the actual environment."*

**Weakness**: adversarial policies can exploit model errors — the agent finds trajectories that look good in the dream but fail in reality, usually by visiting out-of-distribution states where the dynamics model is wrong.

### Why it matters for the thesis

- Establishes the **decoupling** of world model (perception + prediction) from controller (decision making).
- Directly relevant to the chess / board-game angle: can a predictor build an internal simulation and act through it?
- Highly readable; good entry point into model-based RL and latent-space dynamics.

## Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models (DreamerV3)

**Hafner et al., 2023** · [arXiv:2301.04104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104)  
**Notes**: [[Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models - notes]]  
**PDF**: `papers/Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models 2301.04104v2.pdf`

### Summary

A **generalist, robust** world-model-based RL algorithm. With **one fixed hyperparameter configuration**, DreamerV3 outperforms tuned specialist methods on **150+ tasks** spanning 2D, 3D, continuous, and discrete environments (Atari, ProcGen, DM Lab, Control Suite, Minecraft, …). First algorithm to collect **diamonds in Minecraft from scratch** without human data or curricula — a long-standing sparse-reward challenge.

### Why it matters for the thesis

- Shows world models can be **scaled and generalized** across wildly different domains — relevant to game-related modeling (roguelike, deck-building, board games).
- RSSM latent dynamics are a natural object for **thermodynamic / semantic-dynamics analysis** (imagination rollouts, repetitive policies, domain "weight").
- The Minecraft diamond result demonstrates learning long-horizon behaviour purely from sparse rewards via imagined trajectories.

## GAIA-1: A Generative World Model for Autonomous Driving

**Hu et al. (Wayve), 2023** · [arXiv:2309.17080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17080)  
**Notes**: [[GAIA-1 A Generative World Model - notes]]  
**PDF**: `papers/GAIA-1 A Generative World Model for Autonomous Driving 2309.17080v1.pdf`

### Summary

Multimodal **generative world model** for autonomous driving. GAIA-1 combines classical world-model ideas with large-scale **generative video** modelling. The model is partitioned into two components:

1. **World model** — autoregressive transformer on vector-quantized image tokens; reasons about high-level scene components and dynamics in latent space.
2. **Video diffusion decoder** — translates latent representations back into high-fidelity, realistic video frames.

Future prediction is reframed as **next-token prediction** in the spirit of LLMs. Video, text, and actions are all encoded as tokens in a shared embedding space, enabling multimodal prompting and controllable scenario generation.

### Why it matters for the thesis

- Shows how **text and action prompts** can steer a world model — useful for generating dynamic events in games or simulations.
- The world-model / decoder split mirrors the Ha & Schmidhuber decoupling, but at generative-video scale.
- Bridges world models and the LLM paradigm (next-token prediction → emergent scene understanding).

## Emergent World Representations (Othello-GPT)

**Li, Hopkins, Bau, Viégas, Pfister, Wattenberg — ICLR 2023** · [arXiv:2210.13382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13382)  
**Notes**: [[EMERGENT WORLD REPRESENTATIONS]] · explainer: [[Othello - The Gradient]]  
**PDF**: `papers/EMERGENT WORLD REPRESENTATIONS EXPLORING A SEQUENCE MODEL TRAINED ON A SYNTHETIC TASK.pdf`

### Summary

A pleasant, accessible read. Demonstrates **causally** that a GPT trained **only** on Othello move sequences develops an internal, **non-linear** representation of board state — without ever being told the rules, board geometry, or piece positions. The probing methodology is sound and directly relevant to asking whether sequence models (including chess engines) build usable internal world models.

### Why it matters for the thesis

- Closest precedent for the **chess / board-game + emergent world model** direction.
- Methodology (probe → intervene → measure) is a template for studying compressed TinyML models: does quantization destroy the causal representation?
- Bridges world models and interpretability — connects to the semantic-dynamics pillar (diagnosing "stuck" regions in representation space).
