\subsection{Relationship Between \texorpdfstring{\(\Delta_i\) and \(\delta_i\)}{Delta i and delta i}}\label{sec:delta-vs-task}

A crucial distinction in this work is between \textbf{per-sample gradients} \(\Delta_i\) and \textbf{task vectors} \(\delta_i\):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \(\Delta_i = \nabla_{w_{\mathrm{head}}} \mathcal{L}(\hat{f}_{w_{\mathrm{base}}}(s_i), v_i)\) is the gradient of the loss with respect to the head parameters, evaluated at a \textbf{single position} \(s_i\) using the base model. It represents the direction in which the head would need to move to reduce the loss on that specific position.
    \item \(\delta_i = \theta_i - \theta_{\mathrm{base}}\) is the actual change in the head parameters after \textbf{fine-tuning} on all positions in bucket \(\mathcal{D}_i\). It is the accumulated effect of the per-sample gradients over the bucket.
\end{itemize}

The central hypothesis of this work is that clustering positions by their \(\Delta_i\) yields a partition \(\mathcal{P}\) for which the resulting \(\delta_i\) are maximally diverse, meaning each expert specializes in a distinct region of the state space.

\section{Step 1: Train the Base Model}\label{sec:step-base}

\draftnote{Shared dual-POV L1, L2, output head. Soft cross-entropy on WDL labels from a strong teacher (Lc0). This is the chosen loss (not MSE on centipawns or EV).}

\section{Step 2: Compute Sample Gradients}\label{sec:step-gradients}

\draftnote{Per-sample gradients w.r.t.\ head parameters. Normalisation (L2 or standardisation). Storage and compute cost.}

\section{Step 3: Cluster Sample Gradients}\label{sec:step-cluster}

\draftnote{K-Means and alternatives (DBSCAN, Density Peak; some choose \(B\)). How to pick \(B\). Validation: inertia, silhouette, interpretability.}

\section{Step 4: Train the Dispatcher}\label{sec:step-dispatcher}

\draftnote{Input: L1 activations \(h\). Output: bucket id \(b\). Linear layer or small MLP. Cross-entropy. Needed because gradients are not available at inference.}

\section{Step 5: Fine-Tune Expert Heads}\label{sec:step-experts}

\draftnote{Freeze L1; fine-tune one head per bucket from the base model. Result: \(B\) specialised experts.}
