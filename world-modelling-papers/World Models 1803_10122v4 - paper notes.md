### 1. Introduction

They can train the agent entirely inside of its own hallucinated dream generated by its world model, and transfer this policy back into the actual environment.

 They look at training a large neural network to tackle RL tasks, by dividing the agent into a large world model and a small con- troller model.

Unsupervised learning -> 🌍 World model 
🌍 World model -> 🕹 controller model

The world model is unchanged during the RL phase of the training.

 **A small controller lets the training algorithm focus on the credit assignment problem on a small search space**

By training the agent through the lens of its world model, they show that it can learn a highly compact policy to perform its task.
